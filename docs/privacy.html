Cut Tracker privacy policy

Effective 2026-10-19

Cut Tracker is an offline app. It has no account, no server and no internet permission. Nothing you enter leaves your phone unless you export it yourself.

What the app stores

  • The program you set up: dates, weights, calorie and protein targets, meals, exercises, checkpoints, reminder times.
  • What you log each day: which day type you picked, items you checked off, weigh-ins, lift sets, meal picks.
  • Progress photos, if you take them. They are saved in the app's private storage and are never uploaded.
  • Derived numbers such as XP, badges and your chain, computed from the logs above.

All of this lives in app-private storage on the device. Other apps cannot read it.

What the app does not do

  • No analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising, no tracking of any kind.
  • No network access. The app does not request the INTERNET permission, so it cannot send data anywhere.
  • No account, no sign-in, no email collection.

Permissions

  • Camera: only when you choose to take a progress photo. Photos stay on the device.
  • Notifications: one daily weigh-in reminder and one "pick a day" reminder, both optional.
  • Exact alarms: so reminders fire at the time you chose. If you do not allow it, reminders still fire, just less precisely.
  • Vibrate: haptic ticks when you check items, switchable off in Settings.

Backups and export

Settings has Export, which writes a JSON or zip file and hands it to an app you pick (your file manager, Drive, email). Where that file goes is up to you and that app. Android's own device backup may also include the app's data under your Google account settings; you control that in Android Settings, not in Cut Tracker.

Deleting your data

Settings has Reset program, which wipes every log, photo and setting. Uninstalling the app removes everything as well.

Health note

Cut Tracker is a logbook and a scoreboard, not medical advice. The targets you set are yours. Talk to a doctor before a diet if you have a condition, and stop if you feel unwell.
